What the software is (and is not)

  • A local safety and evidence layer for consequential agent-driven commands.
  • Not an operating-system sandbox, job scheduler, compliance certification product, or transparency-log system in the current release candidate.
  • Optional HMAC-SHA256 receipt authentication is a symmetric-key MAC, not an asymmetric digital signature; anyone with the shared key can authenticate or forge a receipt.
  • Optional Ed25519 (runspecimen[ed25519]) enables offline public-key verification; trusted success requires an externally trusted public key, not a key embedded only in the receipt. Soft keys on disk are not absolute non-repudiation.
  • Approval requires a human on a real TTY. The loopback dashboard is read-only and cannot approve or execute runs.
  • A green postflight and valid certificate mean declared assertions passed under recorded provenance — not that a scientific or engineering claim is true.
