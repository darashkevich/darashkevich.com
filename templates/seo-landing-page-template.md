# SEO Landing Page Template

Use this template for new high-intent service or industry pages.

## 1) Metadata
- Title tag (50-60 chars): `Primary keyword | Yahor Darashkevich`
- Meta description (140-160 chars): include problem, outcome, and audience.
- Canonical URL: absolute page URL.

## 2) URL and Heading Structure
- URL slug: short, specific, keyword-led (example: `/zendesk-ai-support-ops/`).
- One H1 only, includes primary keyword naturally.
- H2 sections:
  - Problem
  - Approach
  - Outcomes
  - Who this fits
  - FAQ
  - CTA

## 3) Content Blocks
- Intro (80-120 words): what you do, for whom, expected business impact.
- Proof block:
  - 3-5 concise bullets with defensible outcomes.
  - Avoid unsupported percentages.
- Method block:
  - Clear step-by-step operating model.
- Objection/fit block:
  - Who this is for and not for.

## 4) Internal Links
- Link to homepage, resume page, and at least 1 related service section.
- Add 1 legal link in footer context where appropriate.

## 5) Structured Data
- Include `ProfessionalService` or `FAQPage` schema where relevant.
- Keep values consistent with page content.

## 6) On-Page SEO QA Checklist
- [ ] Title and meta are unique.
- [ ] Exactly one H1.
- [ ] Primary keyword in first 100 words.
- [ ] Images have descriptive `alt` text.
- [ ] No broken internal links.
- [ ] CTA above fold and at bottom.

## 7) Publication Notes
- Submit updated sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Trigger IndexNow notification after deploy.
