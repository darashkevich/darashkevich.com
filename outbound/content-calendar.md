# LinkedIn content calendar (founder-facing)

Publish **one LinkedIn post per week**. Goal: when a founder checks your profile after outreach, they see executive judgment — not job-seeker energy.

Log ideas and publish status in the Sheet **Content** tab (import from [templates/content-posts.csv](templates/content-posts.csv)).

## Positioning for posts

Write as an operator who has scaled customer organizations. Sell:

- Scaling support organizations
- AI-enabled customer operations
- Subscription / eCommerce expertise
- Executive operational leadership

Avoid: tool tutorials as the headline, “open to work” framing, generic hustle posts.

## Weekly content checklist

- [ ] One post drafted mid-week
- [ ] Specific story or metric (Ka'Chava-scale proof where honest)
- [ ] Clear takeaway a founder/COO can use Monday morning
- [ ] Soft CTA optional (exchange notes / comment) — never “DM me for jobs”
- [ ] Published + URL logged in **Content**
- [ ] Best posts reused as Day 21 follow-up material in outreach

## Draft skeleton

1. **Hook** — concrete claim or mistake (1–2 lines)
2. **Context** — stage or pattern (100→500, post–Series B, subscription scale)
3. **Proof** — what you saw or built (numbers when real)
4. **Play** — what to do differently
5. **Close** — invite peer conversation, not applications
